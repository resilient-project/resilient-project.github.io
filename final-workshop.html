About the workshop

Europe must plan grids, industry and energy supply for decades ahead without knowing future fuel prices, technology costs or how fast new infrastructure gets built. RESILIENT has developed open-source tools and methods to address this uncertainty. We applied them in research papers and case studies, taught them in workshops and training events, and contributed them to the open-source PyPSA ecosystem.

At this final workshop, we share what we have learned: the project's main findings, case studies from Germany, Finland and France, and the open data and tools anyone can reuse. A panel weighs the costs and benefits of reducing Europe's dependence on gas imports.
